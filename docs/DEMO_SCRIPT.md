# Three-minute CXOS Demo

## 30-second pitch

“CXOS helps teams notice when an experience is going wrong before the customer gives up. It brings cross-channel history together, explains risk with evidence, recommends the next step, and waits for a person to approve. We’ll follow Sarah from repeated delivery contacts through a simulated rescue, then inspect the outcome and audit trail. Every record here is synthetic, and the AI is clearly labeled Demo AI.”

## Click path

| Time | Action | Talk track |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Open Overview. Point to the loop and health/risk metrics. | “Listen → Understand → Predict → Recommend → Act → Learn. CXOS notices patterns across messy interactions.” |
| 0:20–0:55 | Open Sarah Williams from the at-risk customer list. | “Three contacts across channels, sentiment falling, engagement down, and a refund request. The suspicious instruction in the timeline was blocked as untrusted content.” |
| 0:55–1:25 | Open Action review, approve Sarah's recommendation, then return to Sarah's profile. | “The AI suggests. A person reviews the evidence and approves. Approval does not execute the action.” |
| 1:25–2:05 | Run the demo rescue from Customer 360. | “This is a deterministic simulation: assignment, personalized draft, issue resolution, follow-up scheduling, and updated health estimate. No message or refund is sent.” |
| 2:05–2:35 | Show updated health, sentiment, timeline and response summary. | “The estimated health change is a demo outcome, not proof of retention or revenue impact.” |
| 2:35–3:00 | Open Trust & audit. Show approval and execution rows. | “The system records who approved and what the demo changed. Copilot answers include evidence and limitations.” |

Inbox actions also work for the selected synthetic customer: approve a pending recommendation, generate and edit a draft that remains local to the browser, record an escalation, or resolve one open synthetic issue. Drafts are never sent.

## Backup

If a mutation fails, use **Reset demo** and show the pre-seeded evidence and trust controls. The app does not depend on a live model provider.
